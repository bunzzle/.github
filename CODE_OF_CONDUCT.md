# Code of conduct

Bunzzle is built in public. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests or anywhere else in this organization, is expected to:

- be kind and patient, especially with people who are new;
- criticize ideas and code, never people;
- accept that a maintainer may say no, and explain why when they do.

Not accepted: harassment, insults, personal attacks, discrimination of any kind, sharing someone's private information, and sexual content.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and block people who break these rules. To report a problem privately, email hello@bunzzle.com. Reports are kept confidential.
